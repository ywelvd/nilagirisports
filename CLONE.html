About Nilgiri Sports

Nilgiri Sports is a locally trusted sports and fitness store located in Nilgiri, Balasore, Odisha. We serve students, athletes, schools, coaching centres, clubs, and fitness enthusiasts across Balasore and nearby areas with quality sports equipment and reliable service.

We believe that the right sports gear helps improve performance, safety, and confidence. Our focus is on providing durable, practical, and affordable products that suit Indian playing conditions and the sports culture of Odisha.

Customer satisfaction is our priority. We provide honest guidance, dependable service, and after-sales support, ensuring customers get the right product for their needs. With strong local connections and an understanding of the sports culture of Balasore and nearby areas, Nilgiri Sports continues to grow as a reliable name in the region.
